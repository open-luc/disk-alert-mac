## What's new in 0.5.0
- A drive that won't eject shows which apps are using it, with Quit Apps & Eject, Force Eject or Try Again, and what each one does.
- A drive plugged in while your Mac sleeps alerts once you've unlocked it, so you hear it.
- Drives already plugged in when you install Disk Alert are in your drive list straight away.
- Updates show one progress bar from download to restart, then confirm they're installed.
- Clearer Check for Updates: readable notes, a one-line "up to date", and on an alpha, a way back to the public release.
